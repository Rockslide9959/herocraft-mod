# The Titan (world boss) — reference (v0.14.20)

Not the Titan Shifter hero power (see `TITANSHIFTER_REFERENCE.md`). A very rare Overworld wilderness boss.
Package `com.projecthero.mod.titan` (`TitanConfig`, `TitanThreat`, `TitanSpawner`, `TitanTerrain`,
`TitanHealthCap`) and `com.projecthero.mod.titan.entity` (`TitanEntity`, `DisguisedTitanEntity`,
`TitanBoulderEntity`, `TitanEntityTypes`). Client: `client.render.TitanRenderer` (GeckoLib, registered from
`TitanEntityRenderers`); model, texture, glowmask and animations are generated by
`scratchpad/gen_v01420_titan_model.js`. Config: `config/projecthero_titan.json`
(re-written with any new keys on every start; missing keys/sections take the code defaults).

## Encounter

A `DisguisedTitanEntity` ("Wanderer") spawns on open surface ground, looks like a plain zombie except for a
yellow spark every ~1.5 s, and on death is struck by (visual-only) lightning and replaced by the real
`TitanEntity`: 18 blocks tall (`SCALE = 18 / 1.95`), 1800 HP, armour 10 / toughness 8, full knockback
resistance, no fall damage, detection 150 blocks, follow range 176. It spawns roaring (INTRO animation) and
waits ~2.5 s before its first attack. Loot: `data/projecthero/loot_table/entities/titan.json`.

Movement never runs an A* search (the v0.10.10 lag fix): it steers straight at its target with the move
control, steps up to 10 blocks (STEP_HEIGHT), and `tickUnstick` carves a corridor through anything that
holds it still for half a second. Block drops from destruction are throttled (`blockDropChance`,
`maxBlocksPerDestruction`).

## Moves

Every telegraphed move winds up for **2 s** (`WINDUP_TICKS = 40`) with a distinct animation, sound and
particle tell, then resolves; per-move cooldowns plus a global 16-tick gap between moves. Damage to a
player is capped at 80 % of their max health per hit (`scaleForPlayer`). All area moves hit **every**
valid player in range, not only the target.

| Move | Tell (animation) | Effect | Chosen when | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Punch | right fist cocked back, shoulder turned | 20 to the target within 7 (+1) blocks, knockback | target ≤ 7 | 1 s |
| Backhand Sweep | arm wound far out to the side, then swept low across the front | 22 + hard knockback to everyone in the front 180° within 10 + half-width | target ≤ 12 | 3.5 s |
| Stomp | knee raised high, arms out for balance | 30 + knock-up within 4.5, breaks nearby blocks (never the floor) | target underfoot / ≤ 6 | 4 s |
| Ground Slam | both fists raised overhead, leaning back, then a hammer-fist | 26 within 6, crater | target ≤ 9 | 5.5 s |

## Aggro (v0.14.4, `TitanThreat`)

All vanilla target goals are stripped (including `HurtByTargetGoal`, which was the cause of the one-target
lock). Instead:

- **Damage** a player deals adds `damage × damageThreatMultiplier` threat; standing within
  `proximityRange` (14) of its edge adds up to `proximityThreatPerSecond` (3/s). Threat halves every
  `threatHalfLifeTicks` (10 s).
- Every `retargetIntervalTicks` (2 s), not mid-attack/grab/swat, it scores every valid player within
  detection range as `threat + nearbyWeight × (1 − distance / nearbyRange)` and switches when the best beats
  the current target by `switchMargin` (×1.25). With `randomSwitchChance` (20 %) it instead picks at random
  among players scoring ≥ `randomScoreFloor` (60 %) of the best.
- An invalid target (dead, logged out, creative/spectator, other dimension, past follow range) is replaced
  immediately. Creative/spectator players are never targeted or hit (knockback included).
- A switch is announced with a low growl and angry-villager sparks over the new target's head.

## Model and animation (v0.14.20)

A GeckoLib model of its own (it used to be the vanilla zombie rig scaled up): a hunched, massively muscled giant
zombie -- a hump of trapezius with bone spikes tearing out of the spine, a torn-open ribcage (embers glow inside),
long arms hanging to the knees with huge forearms, oversized black-clawed hands, iron shackles on both wrists and
the left ankle with broken chains, ragged trousers and loin rags, a heavy brow, lower tusks and a jaw that drops
open. Rotting green-grey skin with mottling, necrotic patches and gashes with run-off blood; the eyes, the mouth
and the rib cavity glow (`titan_glowmask.png`, `AutoGlowingGeoLayer`).

- **Assets**: `geo/titan.geo.json`, `textures/entity/titan.png` (512x336, 3 texels per model pixel, painted
  procedurally with shading), `textures/entity/titan_glowmask.png`, `animations/titan.animation.json`. All four
  are written by `node scratchpad/gen_v01420_titan_model.js [previewDir]` -- edit the generator, not the files.
  With a preview directory it also software-renders posed front / side / three-quarter sheets of key frames. It
  fails if a strike keyframe is off its tick or a clip outlasts the server's animation.
- **Scale**: the model is authored in model pixels; `TitanRenderer.MODEL_SCALE` (5.8277, printed by the generator)
  makes the rest pose exactly the 18-block hit-box height. Hit-box, `SCALE` and every gameplay number are
  unchanged. It is wider than the hit-box (~16 blocks arm to arm); the hit-box is the same pillar as before.
- **Sync**: unchanged -- `TitanEntity.Anim` in `DATA_ANIM` (ordinal + bump counter). `DEATH` was appended.
- **Controllers** (`TitanEntity.registerControllers`): `main` plays the synced clip (restarted whenever a new one
  arrives, even the same move twice), otherwise `idle` / `walk` / `run` by the actual horizontal speed (run
  above 0.27 b/t, back to walk below 0.23; measured walk 0.19 and chase 0.49 b/t -- the gait cycles are sized from
  the stride so the feet don't skate). `upper` plays only the Swat and keys just the torso, head and arms, so the
  legs keep walking under it (the later controller wins for the bones it keys).
- **Timing**: `main` blends 4 ticks (`ANIM_TRANSITION`) before a clip's clock starts, so every telegraphed strike
  is keyed at clip tick 36 = server tick 40 (the damage tick). Grab-throw hurls at clip tick 26 (server 30); the
  Swat chops at clip tick 8 on the 2-tick `upper` controller (server 10). `TitanModelV01420GameTests` pins this.
- **Head**: the never-keyed `neck` bone is set absolutely every frame from the look yaw/pitch (so no GeckoLib head
  accumulation), eased to 45 % during aimed attacks and to 0 for the roar, leap and death.
- **Hurt flinch**: the never-keyed `flinch` bone (waist to chest) jerks back over the 10-tick hurt timer.
- **Grab**: the generator solves the hold pose so the closed fist sits where `tickGrab` pins the victim (up 0.62 h,
  forward 1.05 w, right 0.5 w); it roars in their face, winds back and hurls.
- **Boulder**: claws dig in, the rock (the ground block under it, as before) grows out of the ground at clip tick
  14, is heaved overhead between both palms (held 5 blocks apart by the generator) and released on the strike.
  Drawn by `TitanRenderer.HeldBoulderLayer` in the right hand's bone space.
- **Death**: `die()` now plays `DEATH` and the body stays `DEATH_TICKS` (90) instead of 30 -- staggers back
  roaring, drops to its knees (dust + thud), sways, topples face-first (a line of dust and a boom along its facing
  at clip tick 62 + 4), then crumbles to smoke. `tickDeath` is overridden so vanilla's 20-tick removal doesn't cut
  it short. No vanilla tip-over or red death tint.
- **Clips**: idle (breathing, jaw working), walk, run, punch, sweep, stomp, slam, shockwave, grab, grab_throw,
  boulder, charge (+ charge_run knuckle gallop), leap (crouch) / leap_air / leap_land, roar (inhale, then a
  shuddering roar, jaw wide, arms flung out), intro (rises from all fours roaring), swat, death.
- The Disguised Titan is unchanged (vanilla zombie look). `titan_form*` assets belong to the Titan Shifter power.
- Visual check: a temporary client harness (`scratchpad/TitanBossDebugHarness.v01420.java.txt`; copy it to
  `src/client/java/com/projecthero/mod/client/TitanV01420DebugHarness.java`, call `init()` from
  `onInitializeClient`, run `runClient` with `PROJECTHERO_TITAN_DEBUG=1`, optionally
  `PROJECTHERO_TITAN_ONLY=idle,walk,roar,...`) tours every clip and screenshots it into `run/screenshots`.

`threatHalfLifeTicks`, `retargetIntervalTicks`, `nearbyWeight`, `nearbyRange`, `switchMargin`,
`randomSwitchChance`, `randomScoreFloor`. Existing config files pick these up with their defaults (Gson
keeps field initialisers for missing keys); no existing value changed, so no migration is needed.
