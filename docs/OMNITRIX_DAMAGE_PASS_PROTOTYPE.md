# Omnitrix damage pass -- prototype aliens (v0.17.2)

User: "can you make the damage of all of ben 10s alien stronger, i want each alien to feel like its own entire ability and i want
them to be strong aswell please ... make each alien stronger accordingly please?"

New scale: quick hits 8-12, normal moves 20-28, big moves 28-35, damage over time 7-10 a pulse, ultimates 70-110 (old: 5-8 /
12-17 / 17-20 / 40-70). Roughly x1.6, ultimates x1.6-1.8. Cooldowns are unchanged.

Bare-fist melee bonuses (the alien's `attack` stat) are the user's own numbers: Four Arms +10, Diamondhead +8 (+4 more with the
Combat Mode blades, +12 in all), Upgrade +5, Ghostfreak +5, Stinkfly +4, Wildmutt +6, Ripjaws +5, Grey Matter unchanged (+2).
Ghostfreak's Chilling Touch (+2), Upgrade's Circuit Jolt (+2) and Grey Matter's Precision Jab (+2) stay as they were, since the
fist bonus itself carries the raise.
