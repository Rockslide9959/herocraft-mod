Both are 64x64 palette PNGs, wide (Steve) arms. They are never modified; scratchpad/sentry/make_suit_textures.js turns them into
textures/entity/sentry/sentry_comics_* and sentry_void_* (head, hair and bare skin cleared). Those outputs are .gitignored: local builds
only. Without them the game falls back to the mod's own original Sentry / Void textures.
