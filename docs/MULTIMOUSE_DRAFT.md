# Multimouse: the Mouse Miraculous (draft, nothing built yet)

**Mullo**, the Kwami of Multiplication, lives in the **Mouse Pendant**. Multimouse uses a **jump rope** as a whip, a lasso and a grapple. Her special power, **Multitude**, splits her into a swarm of tiny copies of herself.

It works like the other Miraculous:
- **Where it comes from:** a jewel in the Miraculous Box, or a spawn calling.
- **Power slot:** your one Primary power.
- **Transforming:** you say the phrase in chat. You transform with **"Mullo, multiply!"** and change back with **"Divide!"**. The Kwami's name is optional, as with the others.
- **Feeding:** Mullo is hungry after every detransform and eats **cheese** (Plagg's Camembert), or 10 pieces of any other food.
- **Shared bonuses:** +7 melee damage and Regeneration I while transformed.
- **Mullo's behaviour:** follows you everywhere, can't be hurt, and **N** tucks them away.

## Passives (while transformed)
- **Light on her feet:** 75% less fall damage. A mouse survives any fall.
- **Quiet as a mouse:** while you crouch, mobs lose track of you if they are more than 6 blocks away.

## Multitude (Shift+Z)
You cry **"Multitude!"** and split into **8 mini Multimice**: you plus 7 copies, each about a quarter of your size, wearing your own skin and the suit. It lasts **60 s**.
- **You:** tiny, quick and hard to hit, and you can run through 1-block gaps. Your moves still work, at **half damage**.
- **The 7 copies:** they fight beside you, mob them onto whatever you hit or look at, and nibble for 3 damage a bite. Each has 6 health and pops in a puff of pink sparkle when it's killed. The HUD shows how many are left.
- **Recombine:** press Shift+Z again, or wait for the timer to run out. Every surviving copy runs back to you and you snap back to full size in a **shockwave**: knockback to everything within 6 blocks, plus 2 damage for each copy that made it back.
- **Countdown:** as with the other specials, using it starts the countdown to changing back.
