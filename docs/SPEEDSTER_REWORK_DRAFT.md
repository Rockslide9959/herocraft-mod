# Speedster rework — draft (nothing built yet)

Two kinds of speedster that **play differently**:

| | **Speedster** (the mutation, today's Super Speed) | **Speed Force Speedster** (the DC Flash) |
|---|---|---|
| Tier | Mutation (serum / random serums, as now) | **Hero-Tier Primary** (an upgrade, see below) |
| How speed works | **Instant tiers:** tap C for Speed Mode (20 / 40 b/s), V for Overdrive (32 / 120 b/s) | **Momentum:** you're slow off the line and build speed the longer you run unbroken |
| Top speed | 120 b/s (Overdrive sprint) | **200 b/s** at full momentum (the fastest runner in the mod) |
| Energy | none (cooldowns only) | **Speed Force** meter: fills as you run (by distance), spent on moves |
| Look | plain see-through after-images; Overdrive crackles with lightning | gold lightning off the body at all times, growing with momentum, plus a sonic boom ring at 100 b/s |
| Play style | burst brawler: tap a mode and go | builder: keep moving, chain moves without stopping, cash momentum into huge hits |

## How you upgrade (the Speed Force awakening)
You must **already be a Speedster**. Then, **while running in Overdrive**, either get **struck by lightning** or **run through a Charged Copper Plate** / an Overloaded Redstone Coil. The Speed Force takes you, 100% of the time; no 50/50 like today's origin.

You stop being a mutation Speedster and become the Speed Force Speedster (your one Primary). The current Speed Force origin (the 3 potion effects plus lightning) stays as the way to **become a plain Speedster** without a serum.

## Momentum (the core of the Flash)
- **The bar:** Momentum goes 0 to 100%. It rises while you run in a direction and climbs faster the longer the run lasts: about 3 s to 25%, 10 s to 60% and 20 s to 100%.
- **Speed scales with it:** 20 b/s at 0%, 60 at 25%, 120 at 60% and 200 at 100%.
- **What loses momentum:** stopping (gone in about 1.5 s), sharp turns (a little), getting hit (a chunk) and running into walls (most of it). Running on water and up walls keeps it.
- **Breaking the sound barrier:** passing 100 b/s fires a sonic boom ring that knocks back nearby mobs.
- **The suit matters:** without the **Flash Suit**, momentum caps at **60%** and your armour burns as it does today.

## Speed Force energy
- **The meter:** 0 to 100. It fills by distance run (about 1 per 20 blocks) and gains a little from lightning strikes and Charged Copper. It doesn't refill standing still.
- **Uses:** moves spend it, and the big ones also **cash in momentum** (they spend momentum and hit harder the more you had).

## The Flash's moveset (12, all new)
| Key | Move | Shift + key |
|---|---|---|
| **R** | **Speed Punches:** a flurry of blows; damage grows with momentum (4 to 12 a hit) | **Infinite Mass Punch:** spend all your momentum on one punch, 10 + up to 60 at full momentum; huge knockback |
| **G** | **Lightning Throw:** run up, then hurl a bolt of Speed Force lightning (damage grows with momentum) | **Speed Tornado:** run circles to make a cyclone that lifts and spins enemies |
| **X** | **Speed Force Step:** a short burst along your aim that ADDS momentum (for getting started) | **Phase:** hold to vibrate through walls |
| **Z** | **Flash Time:** time slows to a crawl for you (costs Speed Force; lasts longer the more momentum you have) | **Time Remnant:** a copy of you from a second ago runs out and fights for 8 s |
| **V** | **Rescue Run:** grab the ally or mob in front of you and carry them at full speed | **Speed Heal:** burn Speed Force to heal fast and clear bad effects |
| **C** | **Speed Force Surge** (mode): lock momentum at 100% for 15 s with blinding lightning | **Rewind:** snap back to where you stood 5 s ago, with the health you had then (big Speed Force cost) |

**Passives:** water-running and wall-running scale with momentum, faster eating and healing like today, and no fall damage above 25% momentum.

## The Flash Suit HUD (already built)
It already shows velocity, mph, mode and timers. For the Speed Force Speedster it would add the **Momentum** gauge and the **Speed Force** meter.
