# Falcon & Silver Surfer — design (for approval, nothing built yet)

Both follow the house rules: R / G / Z / C are attacks, X is movement, V is utility, every key has a Shift move (12 each),
no H / N moves, ONE Primary power, a framed HUD with a meter, and guide + I-menu pages.

---

## FALCON (Sam Wilson) — craftable gear

**The gear** (you're Falcon while wearing all of it; take it off and you're not):
| Piece | Slot | Recipe idea |
|---|---|---|
| **EXO-7 Wing Pack** | chest | Elytra + 2 Netherite Scrap + 4 Iron Ingots + 2 Phantom Membranes |
| **Falcon Goggles** | head | 2 Glass Panes + 2 Leather + Redstone + Gold Ingot |
| **Flight Suit** (legs + boots) | legs / feet | Leather + Iron + Red / Grey Dye |
| **Redwing** (drone) | item, spawned by the pack | Comes with the Wing Pack |

Wearing it shows the **Planet Minecraft "The Falcon (Original Avengers)" skin** as a suit model over your own skin (the Ladybug / Nova overlay technique), plus real 3D wings on your back: folded when you walk, spreading when you fly.

**Meter: Thrust** (fuel for boosts, missiles and the Redwing). It refills while you glide or stand on the ground.

**Flight — the fun part (a physics wingsuit, not hover flight):**
- **Take off:** double-tap Space. The wings snap open with a jet burst.
- **Glide with momentum:** dive (look down) to gain speed, pull up to trade that speed for height. Going too slow makes you stall and drop.
- **Hold Sprint:** the jet packs burn Thrust for speed. **Space:** a wing-flap climb burst.
- **Banking:** the camera tilts into turns. Skim the ground for a speed bonus.
- **Landing:** a hero landing with a dust ring (no fall damage while geared).

**Moveset:**
| Key | Move | Shift + key |
|---|---|---|
| **R** | **Wrist Guns:** hold for twin SMG bursts from the wrist launchers (fast, low damage) | **Micro-Missile Volley:** 4 wing-mounted missiles lock onto up to 4 targets in your crosshair cone |
| **G** | **Wing Slash:** the steel wings cut in an arc (in the air it's a diving slash that carries you through the target) | **Wing Shield:** fold the wings in front of you to block projectiles; let go to bash and knock back |
| **X** | **Afterburner:** a directional jet burst (on the ground it's a boosted leap that becomes flight) | **Barrel Roll:** a spinning evade with brief invulnerability that also reflects arrows |
| **Z** | **Redwing — Strike:** your drone flies to the target and peppers it with lasers (it follows you otherwise) | **Redwing — Bombing Run:** the drone dives on the aimed area and drops micro-bombs plus an EMP that stuns |
| **V** | **Tactical Goggles:** marks every enemy within 60 blocks through walls, with zoom and distance readouts (toggle) | **Lift & Carry:** grab an ally or mob in front of you and fly with them (drop to throw) |
| **C** | **Dive Bomb:** from height, fold the wings and plunge; damage and shockwave scale with your speed | **Talon Drop:** snatch an enemy, rocket straight up, and let go at the top |

Passives: no fall damage in the gear, +20% sprint speed, and Redwing auto-scans for enemies as you fly.

## SILVER SURFER (Norrin Radd) — the Power Cosmic

**Getting it:** right-click the **Silver Surfer Board** (Galactus's reward). The Power Cosmic floods in with a flash and the board bonds to you. You become the Surfer: the **Silver Surfer player model** (the same skin the Galactus event's Surfer uses), chrome sheen and glow included. One Primary power, as always.

**Meter: Power Cosmic.** It refills fast under open sky and slowly underground or indoors.

**Flight — the fastest in the game:**
- **Double-tap Space:** the board flies up under your feet and you surf it. There's a lean / crouch pose, and the board banks into turns.
- **Cruise:** 60 blocks/s. **Hold Sprint:** 110 blocks/s.
- **Light-Speed (Shift+X):** a few seconds at **150 blocks/s**, faster than anything else in the mod. Kryptonian boost is 55 blocks/s, and Super Speed's Overdrive sprint is 120 on the ground.
- A silver streak and cosmic sparkle trail follow you, plus a sonic-boom ring when you break into Light-Speed.

**Moveset:**
| Key | Move | Shift + key |
|---|---|---|
| **R** | **Power Cosmic Blast:** a hand-blast bolt (hold to channel a beam) | **Cosmic Barrage:** 8 homing orbs of cosmic energy |
| **G** | **Board Strike:** throw the board as a spinning boomerang that chains through enemies and comes back (in flight it's a ram charge) | **Transmutation:** turn the target to solid silver (frozen 5 s); the next hit shatters it for bonus damage. Bosses are only slowed |
| **X** | **Cosmic Dash:** a 30-block blink-dash along your aim, leaving a silver streak (hits what it passes) | **Light-Speed:** the 150 blocks/s burst (needs the board) |
| **Z** | **Cosmic Barrier:** hold up a shimmering shield that blocks and reflects projectiles | **Molecular Phase:** go intangible for 4 s (walk through walls on your own level, can't be hit) |
| **V** | **Cosmic Awareness:** see every creature within 100 blocks and nearby ores through walls (toggle) | **Healing Touch:** heal yourself and allies around you, and cure poison, wither and the like |
| **C** | **Cosmic Nova:** a huge expanding detonation of Power Cosmic around you | **Herald's Meteor Storm:** call meteors down on the aimed area for 5 s |

Passives: immune to fall, fire, drowning and suffocation, no hunger drain, Regeneration I, and you breathe anywhere.
