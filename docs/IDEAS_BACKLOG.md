Ideas the user liked enough to keep for later (not scheduled). Pick from here when planning a release.

## Miraculous -- closer to the show (proposed 2026-10-19, v0.16.7; secret identity + Kwami chatter were built then)

- **Luck & misfortune passives**: Ladybug a small good-luck passive (more crits, better drops); Cat Noir a bad-luck aura --
  enemies near him sometimes trip, miss, or their arrows go wide.
- **Kwami power-ups (Season 3)**: feed your Kwami a special power-up food to unlock a themed form for a while -- e.g.
  **Aqua** (water breathing, fast swimming, aqua suit recolour), **Ice** (frost-themed hits). Bigger feature.
- **Lending a Miraculous**: Guardian-style -- hand your jewel to a squadmate for one fight; they become that hero until
  they detransform, then it returns to you automatically.
- **Transformation sequences**: a short unique cinematic per hero -- the camera pulls back, the suit forms piece by piece
  in their colours, like the show's transformation scenes.
