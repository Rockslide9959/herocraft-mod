# v0.13.22 mutation revamp — batch C

Powers: **07 Electrokinesis**, **14 Sonic Scream**, **20 Energy Absorption**, **21 Shockwave Manipulation**,
**24 Wind Manipulation**. Every power now has 8 keys (R, G, X, Z, V, C, H, N = slots 1–8). Numbers are about +20% damage,
−15% cooldowns and +15% resource capacity over the pre-revamp kits, and all stay below the Hero-Tier powers.

Code: handlers in `hero/power/p07|p14|p20|p21|p24`, shared particle geometry + save migration in
`hero/revamp/BatchCFx`, registration (entity type, overlay flags, visual values, HUD meters, per-tick upkeep, server-stop
cleanup) in `hero/revamp/RevampBatchC`, client poses / overlays / tornado renderer / HUD recolour in
`client/mutation/RevampClientC` + `client/mutation/batchc/WindTornadoRenderer`. Textures are generated by
`scratchpad/gen_v01322_c_textures.js`; lang by `scratchpad/lang_v01322_c.js`. Tests: `RevampBatchCGameTests`.

Stale keys from the old kits (Sonic `senses`, Energy `field_bar`, Wind `blade_charge` / `flight` / the `wind_flight`
toggle, Shockwave `pulse_*`) are purged from saves by the passive tick (`BatchCFx.purge`), so an old reserve bar can
never reappear on the HUD.

## 07 Electrokinesis — static stacks

**Signature.** Every Electrokinesis hit adds a **static stack** to its target (max 3, 8 s, refreshed by each new stack).
Stacked targets crackle with sparks (and an orbiting blue ring at 2+ stacks, a double ring at 3) that everyone sees,
take **+10% Electrokinesis damage per stack**, and on reaching **3 stacks are stunned for 1.5 s** (Slowness X, Weakness III,
Mining Fatigue III, AI navigation stopped; then immune to a re-stun for 4 s). Each stack gain also leaves the old
"static shock" Slowness II for 2 s. Resource: charge cell **1150** (was 1000), refills in ~40 s, ~9 s in a
thunderstorm, instantly near a lightning strike. Still `PLAYER_ATTACK` damage (armour applies) — weaker than Thor.

## 20 Energy Absorption — release what you absorbed

**Signature.** Meter **575** (was 500). Fire, lightning, explosion and magic damage soak **60%** into the meter
(**80%** in Absorption Mode) and **set its element**; any other hit soaks **25%** (40% in the mode) and leaves the element
alone. Everything you fire comes out as the element:

* **fire** (orange) — `IN_FIRE` damage, ignites 4 s
* **lightning** (blue) — Slowness VII 1 s stun + an arc to one more enemy within 5 for 50%
* **explosion** (yellow) — extra knockback and lift
* **magic** (purple) — `indirectMagic` damage, Wither I 3 s + Weakness 4 s
* raw (cyan) — plain

Redstone (standing on it, harvesting dust, Sneak+use a block/dust, Energy Drain on redstone) charges it as lightning.
Trickle regen to 25% after 10 s idle; 80%+ gives Strength/Speed; full meter hurts slowly. An empty meter resets to raw.

## 21 Shockwave Manipulation — kinetic storage

**Signature.** Stores **kinetic** force only (never fire/lightning/magic — that is Energy Absorption; never muscle —
that is Super Strength). Gauge **115** (was 100), shown as a segmented GAUGE. Taken damage:

* melee — 15% dampened, **1×** banked
* falls — 40% dampened, **1.2×** banked (not while a Recoil Jump no-fall window is open)
* explosions — halved, **1.5×** banked
* while bracing (holding C) everything banks **2×** and melee is 40% dampened
* while Repulsion Field is up, all damage −30%

Waves spend Kinetic to hit harder, and every wave leaves pale distortion rings + POOF rings (+ GUST at the fist and
kicked-up ground debris).

## Unverified in-game (no client run here)

* **Glide feel**: the server steers a gliding player by sending a motion packet every tick; on a real client this may
  feel slightly rubber-bandy on high ping. Sprint-jump auto-glide only triggers with 3+ blocks of air below, so normal
  sprint-jumping on flat ground is unaffected.
* **Tornado riding**: the rider sits (vanilla riding pose, overridden by `p24.ride`); server-authoritative movement
  with a 1-tick update interval — should be smooth but untested visually. Texture wrap (UV scroll) assumed REPEAT.
* **Overlays**: all shells / eyes / rings / fists are registered and compile, but their look (alpha levels, emissive
  brightness) has not been seen in a client.
* **Electro Bolt Form** uses `teleportTo` — on a laggy connection the client may see a small snap.
* Pose angles for the new `pNN.*` poses were written by hand from the framework's conventions, not tuned in-game.
