v0.17.3 shared flight feel (client.flight.FlightFeel), after Andy's flight. Films are 10 fps (one frame every 2 ticks = real time).
Each power flies the same scripted path: hover (0-1.2 s), cruise (to 3 s), sprint (to 7 s) with a hard right turn at 4-5.5 s,
a pull-up then a dive (5.5-7 s), slow down and slide right (7-9.5 s), hover. The flier is a harness fake player (default skin).
