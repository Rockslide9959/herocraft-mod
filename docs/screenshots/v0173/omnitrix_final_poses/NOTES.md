# Omnitrix final poses: ours vs show (v0.17.3)

Each `<alien>_final_pose.png` has ours on the left and the show on the right. Each half was taken at its own best frame
(the tick is printed in the label). `all_final_poses.png` puts all 12 on one page.
Sources: `heatblast_v2`, `four_arms`, and the `*_v1b` `_vs_show.mp4` films in `../omnitrix_sequences/`.
The final pose is the last held hero pose after the white flash and before the green wash-out or the world cut.

## Quick ranking
- **Close:** Heatblast, Cannonbolt
- **Fair (framing or facing only):** Wildmutt, Ghostfreak, XLR8, Upgrade
- **Off (the pose itself differs):** Four Arms, Diamondhead, Ripjaws, Stinkfly, Wildvine, Grey Matter

Shared pattern: ours tend to be upright, symmetrical and square to the camera. The show's final poses are wide, asymmetric
"power stances" (limbs spread, torso twisted or arched, head tipped back), seen from a low or tilted camera.
