v0.17.3 Andy -- Bone Sword v2: hold V to draw (struggle, rip, grip, burst), Shift+V regen speed, tap V Tear Off, sword-kit VFX,
vanilla sword hold, blade drips. Filmed in a dev client (temporary harness, kept as scratchpad/andy/BoneSwordHarness.v0173.java.txt,
removed from src). 854x480, one frame per game tick: NAME.mp4 is real time (20 fps), NAME_slow.mp4 half speed, NAME_sheet.png a
contact sheet (frame numbers drawn on). The dev skin is random per run (Steve / Alex).

fp_draw / tp_draw_front / tp_draw_side
                             Hold V. Frame 5 V goes down; from frame 10 (past the 5-tick tap window) the sword hand reaches across
                             and grips the off arm, both arms shaking, the wrenches getting harder; the V box fills red and a
                             "Bone Sword nn%" meter counts up. Blood seeps and spurts at the shoulder. Frame 45 (2 s) the arm tears
                             off, is swung round in front and gripped hard (it shakes and swells, blood runs down it). Frame 59 it
                             bursts (blood, bone and flesh) and the sword pops out, then settles into the held pose. Drips start.
fp_drips / tp_drips          The sword held for 8 s: drops swell at the end of each blood run, the tip and the guard, hanging toward
                             the ground whichever way the blade points, then let go, fall and splat (about one drop every 10 ticks).
kit_tp / kit_fp              The sword kit with the new VFX: Bone Slash x3 (a crescent swoosh of blood with a white-hot leading
                             edge; forehand, backhand, wide finisher), Marrow Thrust (spear streak + shock ring), Blood Edge Wave
                             (a flying crescent of blood raining drops), Rib Cage Guard (ring along the ground, bone and blood off
                             the chest, the ribs), Bone Splinter Volley (muzzle cone of bone dust and blood), Parry (white star on
                             the blade) and a parried hit (bigger flash, sparks, the counter's cut), Blood-jet Dash (curtain of blood
                             along the path, the stump's jet), Impale Throw (overhead swoosh; the arm then regrows).
tear_tp / tear_fp            Tap V: Tear Off. On fire and poisoned with a hurt left leg: the hand wrenches the leg off and flings it
                             aside; the fire and poison go with it. Then, unhurt, the off arm is torn off instead.
