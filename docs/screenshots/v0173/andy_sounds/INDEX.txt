Andy sounds, v0.17.3 (projecthero:andy.*). Synthesised for the mod (scratchpad/synth_andy_sounds.js: filtered noise, stick-slip creaks through body resonances, rising-pitch bubbles, click clusters; no samples) plus a few vanilla files re-pitched as extra takes.
andy_sound_reel.mp4 - listening reel (3:08, black frame with the event name on screen): every event with all its takes; the six loops (bone push, muscle, skin, blood jet, boost burn, punch stretch) play two takes twice round each so the seam can be heard; last, a simulated Normal-speed regrowth mixed the way AndySounds layers it (bone 0-3 s, muscle 2-6 s, skin 4-8.5 s, random cracks / squelches / stretches, the wet snap at the end). Rebuild: node scratchpad/andy_sound_reel.js <ffmpeg7.exe> <out.mp4>. (Its regrowth section is the OLD regrowth -- see v2 below.)
andy_sound_reel_v2.mp4 - regrowth v2 reel (1:48), after the tester said the regrowth "sounds like water boiling or lava": only the andy.regrow.* events (bone push/crack, muscle, squelch, skin, skin stretch, finish) with all their takes, then three simulated regrowths at the new AndySounds levels (Normal twice with different random takes, then Fast 3.4 s). No bubbles, fizz, hiss or crackle beds any more: the muscle/skin loops are sparse slurps, sinew/leather creaks, wet ticks, reverse-peel tacks and seals over near-silence. Rebuild: node scratchpad/andy_sound_reel.js <ffmpeg7.exe> <out.mp4> andy.regrow.
