Project Hero v0.14.4 -- One Power, Many Changes

ONE POWER AT A TIME
- You now hold ONE Primary power. Any new hero power, lifting Mjolnir included, replaces what you had. Old saves keep their newest hero.

THOR
- Your lightning, beam, chain, Thunderclap and hammer never hurt your squad (or their pets).
- Thor's Armour forges onto you piece by piece, each piece with its own bolt from the sky.
- Every move is animated, with thick forking lightning and shockwave rings.

MOON KNIGHT
- 3 lunar states: Day x0.7, Night x1.0, Full Moon x1.5 (Nether/End = day).
- Vengeance: more per kill, regenerates 0.5%/s out of combat.
- Half fall damage, 1-block step assist, longer Dash, smooth Cape Glide near the floor.
- Moonbeam: 35 dmg area blast, 5 s cooldown, 10% Vengeance.
- Eye of Khonshu: 1 minute, 30 blocks, buffs you, weakens foes, rains Moonbeams.
- Khonshu's Judgement: 15 s burn (10/s) that heals you, 20 s cooldown.
- Moon Mark 5 s cooldown. Crescent Fan: 5 homing darts. Crescent Slam 18. Grapple Kick 20 with aim assist + lock-on.
- Grappling Line pulls in squad-mates.
- Truncheon: C summons it, 7 damage, 3-hit animated combo, Staff Spin 15.
- Steven Grant: no cape, Fortune III on everything he mines.
- Jake's Vanish: no time limit (Sneak+V again to end) and no mob can target him while invisible.

MAX STEEL
- Jump straight from Normal form into any Turbo mode.
- Turbo recharges in Normal form (bar shown), HUD just says "Normal".
- Slower Turbo recharge (10/s, 5/s in combat).
- Strength Mode has Resistance I again.

GREEN LANTERN
- Simpler HUD like before (no framed panel); wheel border removed.
- Carry Platform riders sit ON the platform.

SYMBIOTE
- A free Symbiote can take over animals, wolves and cats.
- A free Symbiote hunts for your tamed wolf or cat -- or share yours (Sneak + right-click it, suited). Your pet becomes the host: still yours, still sits and follows.
- In a fight it transforms pixel by pixel: 30% bigger, white eyes, tougher, regenerating. ~9 s after, it changes back.
- Pet powers: Tendril Lash, Pounce, Spike Burst, a pinning Latching Bite, and Guardian Shroud (wraps YOU in the Symbiote when you drop low).

HULK
- While a Hulk rampages, his squad can fight back.

TITAN BOSS
- Fully animated, with readable wind-ups.
- New moves: Leaping Slam (jump the shockwave!) and Grave Roar (pulls you in, raises Husks).
- Real aggro: it turns on whoever hurts it most.

RAIDS & GRAVEBOUND
- Raids are repeatable: Pillager Spies can mark a village again after a raid, even with its villagers dead.
- Cursed Graves rekindle; a curse near another raid comes back instead of vanishing.
- Grave Champion and Empowered Zombie heads are wearable, placeable trophies with new 3D models, glowing eyes and a kill record.
- Old configs get the intended Grave Essence drop rates.

Guidebook and ability menus updated with all of the above.
