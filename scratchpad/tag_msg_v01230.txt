v0.12.30 - Wolverine returns to its original form (bone claws removed)

WOLVERINE
- The Bone Claw progression added in 0.12.25 has been removed entirely. Wolverine works exactly as it did before: own Super Regeneration, craft an Adamantium Serum (4 diamonds, 2 netherite ingots, 2 blaze powder, golden apple) and use it to become Wolverine with your adamantium claws straight away.
- Removed: Bone Claw Serum, the hold-to-use upgrade Adamantium Serum, the Bone Claw model and texture, the claw tiers, and the locked Adamantium Execution. All abilities, damage values and controls are back to the original kit.

KEPT FROM 0.12.25
- Symbiote bonding minigame: stop the sweeping marker inside the green zone three times running to bond; miss and you must wait 5 seconds to retry, nothing is used up.
- Green Lantern Mining Drill breaks a 2x2 patch every 0.75 seconds.
- Super Regeneration healing no longer slows down in combat.
- The Hero Pack guide reopens on the page you left it on.

Please wait about 20 minutes for CurseForge to verify the update.
