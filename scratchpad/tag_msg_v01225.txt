v0.12.25 - Wolverine claw progression, Symbiote bonding minigame, Green Lantern drill, guide remembers your page

WOLVERINE
- Wolverine no longer starts with claws. New progression: Wolverine -> Bone Claw Serum -> Bone Claws -> Adamantium Serum -> Adamantium Claws. Both stages are permanent, survive death and relogging, and are visible to other players.
- The old "Adamantium Serum" that turns Super Regeneration into Wolverine is now the Wolverine Serum (same recipe: 4 diamonds, 2 netherite ingots, 2 blaze powder, golden apple).
- Bone Claw Serum (bone blocks, netherite scrap, gold ingots, glass bottle, ghast tear): only works on a Wolverine with no claws. Grows six natural bone claws (3 per hand) with a burst of particles and a healing pulse.
- Bone Claws are a brand-new model: ivory, thick-based, curved, organic and slightly fanned. Weaker than adamantium: 9 bare-handed, Claw Slash 15, Cross Slash 10+10, Claw Dash 10, Frenzy 6 each.
- Adamantium Serum (4 netherite ingots, diamond block, 2 gold blocks, nether star, glass bottle): hold right-click for 3 seconds while the adamantium bonds to your bones (you are rooted and regenerating; letting go cancels without using it). The bone model is then replaced by the silver adamantium claws with the full kit (12 / Slash 18 / Cross 12+12 / Dash 12 / Frenzy 8) and Adamantium Execution unlocks.
- Adamantium Execution is locked with Bone Claws ("Adamantium Claws required."). H shows "Bone Claws required." until you have claws. Berserker Rage works with either claws. Keys are unchanged.
- The Serums refuse with a clear message if you are not Wolverine / lack Bone Claws / already have adamantium, and never get used up when refused. Wolverine HUD shows which claws you have. Admins: /wolverine claws tier none|bone|adamantium.
- Existing Wolverines keep their adamantium claws.

SYMBIOTE
- Bonding now starts with a minigame: stop a sweeping marker inside the green zone three times running (faster and tighter each round) with Space or a click. Miss and the Symbiote recoils (nausea, 5 s before you can retry) and nothing is used up. Win and the usual 35-second bonding debuff follows. Works for wild Symbiotes and filled vials. The server re-checks every press, so it cannot be faked.

GREEN LANTERN
- The Mining Drill now breaks a 2x2 patch of blocks every 0.75 seconds.

SUPER REGENERATION
- The passive healing no longer slows down in combat: a steady 4 HP/s.

HERO PACK GUIDE / MENUS
- The guide now reopens on the page (and scroll position) you left it on instead of jumping back to the overview.
- Wolverine, Symbiote, Green Lantern and Super Regeneration entries updated for all of the above, plus the new items in the Superheroes creative tab.

Please wait about 20 minutes for CurseForge to verify the update.
