Project Hero v0.14.1 — the Mutation Revamp

ALL 27 MUTATIONS REBUILT
• Every mutation now has 8 abilities: the six keys plus two new ones on H (Utility 1) and N (Utility 2). 216 abilities in total.
• Each power has its own signature mechanic, for example:
  – Super Strength throws anything
  – Laser Vision's beam sharpens as it heats
  – Geokinesis uses the ground under you as ammo
  – Crystalkinesis plants crystal nodes, then shatters them
  – Pyrokinesis gets stronger the hotter it runs
  – Cryokinesis freezes targets solid
  – Electrokinesis stacks static on enemies
  – Energy Absorption fires back whatever you absorbed
  – Telekinesis juggles objects in orbit
  – Shadow Manipulation lets you travel as a shadow
  – Wind lets you glide on air currents
• Slightly stronger overall: about +20% damage, −15% cooldowns, +15% resource bars.
• Every move now has a body animation that other players see too.
• New looks: stone, crystal, frost, flame, metal and shadow skins, glowing eyes, a stretching rubber arm, crystal nodes, a rideable tornado, thorn sentries, shadow servants, mirror images, Ice Blade and Hard-Light Blade swords.
• Each power has its own HUD bars in four styles.

KEYS
• H / N fire your mutation's new abilities when no hero power uses those keys. Alt+H / Alt+N always fire them.
• Shift+H opens the power wheel while a mutation is selected.
• Sneak+N fires a COMBO MOVE when you own a matching pair of mutations. There are 10: Meteor Slam, Colossus Stomp, Boulder Barrage, Thunder Sprint, Storm Surge, Glacier Flood, Comet Dash, Fire Tornado, Solar Lance and Umbral Leap.

EVERY MUTATION CAN BE OBTAINED
• Elasticity can now be obtained: fall 10+ blocks onto a slime block, or use a Gravity Distortion Rig.
• Water Manipulation takes 15 s underwater (was 60 s), and the serum keeps you breathing.
• Telekinesis and Teleportation reagents no longer share a recipe. Teleportation now uses Chorus Fruit.
• A serum drunk while you're at mutation capacity no longer costs you the Symbiote or a hero power.
• A pending serum is no longer lost to milk, Purge, a totem, a suit transformation, death or relogging.
• The newest serum you drink always replaces the pending one.
• Fixed the Cryokinesis powder-snow trigger.
• 6 new lab devices: Charged Copper Plates, Crystal Test Chamber, Enchanting Resonance Array, Controlled Blast Chamber, Resonant Chamber and Gravity Distortion Rig. Every device now also works on right-click.
• Research sites now hold more devices. Meteor sites have enough amethyst for Crystalkinesis.
• Mutagenic and Prismatic serums follow the same power-slot rules as everything else.

GREEN LANTERN
• The Power Ring is now a tiny glowing ring on your finger instead of a bracelet, with a new texture.
• Carry Platform has no time limit and no cooldown. It stays until you dismiss it.

Full details are in docs/revamp in the repository.
