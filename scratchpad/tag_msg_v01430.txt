Project Hero v0.14.30 -- Iron Man: Auto-Aim, Flight Drain, Suitcase Docking

🎯 AUTO-AIM ON EVERY MOVE
- Whatever your suit's targeting system has locked onto, your abilities now aim at it automatically.
- Newly aimed: Flares, Sonic Clap, the Flamethrower, the Wrist Laser, the Mark 7 wheel's Micro-Missiles, Homing Missiles (they pick your locked target first) and the Repulsor Dash (you dash at the target).
- Repulsors, the Unibeam, Rockets, Miniguns, Micro-Missiles and the Shoulder Barrage already aimed at the lock.
- Works on every suit with targeting (Mark 2 up, helmet on and faceplate closed). With nothing locked, moves fire where you're looking.

✈️ FLIGHT ENERGY
- Mark 2: flight drains a flat 2 energy per second.
- Mark 3: flight drains a flat 2 energy per second.
- Mark 4: flight drains a flat 1 energy per second.
- Mark 5: flight drains a flat 1 energy per second.
- The flat drain covers hovering, flying, sprint-flying and supersonic. The spec sheet and Fabricator show it.

💼 MARK 5 SUITCASE + SUIT PLATFORM
- Sneak + right-click a Suit Platform ("retrieve") while carrying a Mark 5 Suitcase: the case unfolds into the Mark 5 armour on the platform.
- Right-clicking the platform with the case in your hand, or shift-clicking the case in the platform screen, does the same.
- The platform needs four free Mark 5 slots.

🦾 IRON MAN ARMOUR GOES ON THE IRON MAN WAY
- Iron Man armour can no longer be equipped like normal armour: no dragging it into armour slots, no shift-clicking, no hot-key swapping, no right-click equipping, and dispensers just drop it.
- Suit up with C, or deploy it (Suit Platform, Call Armour, the Mark 7 pod, the Mark 5 suitcase). Taking pieces off by hand still works.
- Loose Mark 5 pieces: with all four in your inventory, right-click one to pack them into the suitcase.

🍗 AUTO-FEED
- The suit now feeds you whenever your hunger bar isn't full, even at 19 of 20, using the best food you're carrying (most filling first).
- Still never eats raw meat or fish, food with side effects, chorus fruit, suspicious stew or golden apples. Mark 3 and up.
