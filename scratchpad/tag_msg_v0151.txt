🦾 IRON MAN ARMOUR RULES
- Iron Man armour can't be taken off like normal armour any more: no picking it out of your armour slots, shift-clicking it out or hot-key swapping it away. It comes off with C (suit down), a Suit Platform retrieve or a send-home. (It already couldn't be put on by hand.)
- Every Iron Man suit is bulletproof: guns (player guns and the Syndicate's gunmen) do nothing to you while the chestplate is on, and never drain integrity or energy.
- Every Iron Man suit now gives 70% knockback resistance.
- Repulsor blasts reach 50 blocks (tap, charged, the hand repulsor and the Remote Pilot drone).
- Mark 3 and up: the mob highlight switches on by itself when you put the helmet on. (The Mark 6 and 7 can still turn it off; it comes back next time the helmet goes on.)
- Marks 3, 6 and 7 now repair 1 integrity per second while worn (the Mark 4 keeps 3/s and the Mark 5 2/s).
- No more two-leg kick when you start flying: you go straight into the flight pose.
- The arc reactor on your chest sits higher, just under the second pixel of the chest.

🔊 IRON MAN SOUND EFFECTS
- Every Iron Man move has its own sound now, built from layered Minecraft sounds:
- Repulsors: a rising charge whine, a concussive "thoom" blast, a heavier boom for the charged shot.
- Unibeam: a deep humming beam with crackle, power-up and power-down.
- Missiles and rockets: pod opening, launch hiss and whoosh, detonations.
- Miniguns: spin-up, rattling fire, spin-down.
- Flares, the sonic clap boom, the wrist laser sizzle, shield hum and deflects, the dash, supersonic flight, the Mark 6 Arc Reactor Surge, JARVIS scan chirps, gauntlet blades, the flamethrower, target lock beeps, HUD on/off, suit stored and power failure.
- The Mark 1 sounds cruder and clunkier than the later suits.
- Every sound has a subtitle, and nearby players hear them too.

💼 MARK 5 SUITCASE
- You now carry the Mark 5 Suitcase like a real briefcase: hanging by its handle at your side instead of sticking out in front of you.

🦯 KINGPIN'S CANE
- The cane now has a proper 3D model when held: a black lacquered shaft with a silver collar and tip and a big faceted diamond head (still a flat icon in your inventory).
- New swing animations that chain together: an Overhead Strike, then a Side Swipe, then a Cane Thrust. Pause for two seconds and the chain starts over.
- They play in first and third person, and the Kingpin swings his cane the same way in his boss fight.
- Damage and the hidden barrel shot are unchanged.

💚 GREEN LANTERN
- The Protective Dome (Sneak + Z) now has a real model: a glowing hard-light globe with a lattice of light that grows out around you, a bright band sweeping up it, and a flicker when it's close to breaking. No more particle rings.

📖 GUIDEBOOK
- New Iron Man "Armour Rules" page and a Green Lantern "Protective Dome" page.
